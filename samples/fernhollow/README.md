# Fernhollow: a sample company Cortex

Fernhollow is a fictional Series B company: scheduling and dispatch software for home-service businesses, about 60 people, five execs and a Chief of Staff, a three-person board, and a Series C planned to close in April 2027. It describes no real company or person.

It's a complete company Cortex on the Markdown backend, with the [Company Pack](../../spec/company-pack.md) installed. Use it to test Cortex skills, the intake skill, briefs, and meeting prep before touching a real client's Cortex.

## What's in it

- A Map with `pack.company: 1`, a Members table, split Company and Exec locations, routing, and a glossary
- 43 records across People, Companies, Departments, Initiatives, Company Commitments, Org, Decisions, Metrics, Board, Risks, and the Inbox
- Three months of readings (July to September 2026) for five Metrics
- A Dream Journal with two nights and questions routed to the reviewer
- An Ingest Log covering the intake interviews, documents, a meeting, and two dreams
- An empty Private space per member, on purpose

## The tensions built in

- **A slipped-commitment pattern.** The pricing page slipped twice, and the VP Sales finalists slipped once. Both wait on a sales decision.
- **A department in trouble.** Sales is Red: no VP Sales since July, pipeline coverage down from 3.1x to 2.2x.
- **An open board ask.** The board asked for a churn cohort analysis on Aug 19. It's due in the Nov 18 pack.
- **A raise in six months,** with NRR sliding from 112% to 106% and runway at 17 months.

## Ten questions it should answer

Ask as Maya (CEO) unless noted. Each answer should cite the records listed.

| # | Question | The answer should say | Records |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Which department is in the worst shape? | Sales, Red: no VP Sales since July, coverage 2.2x | Departments: Sales; Metrics: Pipeline coverage |
| 2 | What did the board ask for that's still open? | Churn cohort analysis (Hana, due Nov 11) and two VP Sales finalists (Maya, due Nov 15) | Board: Board 2026-08-19; the two Company Commitments |
| 3 | What keeps slipping, and why? | The pricing page (twice) and the VP Sales finalists (once); both wait on a sales decision | Company Commitments; Dream Journal pattern |
| 4 | How is retention trending? | NRR 112% to 109% to 106% (July to September); logo churn 1.1% to 1.9% monthly | Metrics: Net revenue retention, Gross logo churn; Metric Values |
| 5 | How much runway do we have? | 17 months at Sept 30 | Metrics: Runway |
| 6 | What's the status of the v2 migration? | Three weeks behind the Dec 15 target; revised cutover plan due Nov 3 | Initiatives: Platform v2 Migration |
| 7 | Who owns the Q1 hiring plan and when is it due? | Theo Lindqvist, Nov 20; needs each exec's ask by Nov 6 | Company Commitments: Deliver the Q1 hiring plan |
| 8 | Where are we with Brightside? | Largest customer, 9% of ARR, renewal Jan 31; plan sent Oct 16 (kept); a Watching risk | Companies: Brightside Home Services; Risks: Brightside concentration |
| 9 | What's the prep status for the November board? | Drafting; pack due Nov 11; two open asks | Board: Board 2026-11-18 |
| 10 | As Priya (Exec), what do I owe and when? | SOC 2 Type I kickoff by Nov 30. She can read Company and Exec records; nothing Private | Company Commitments: Kick off the SOC 2 Type I audit |

A good answer also refuses one thing: asked "What did Maya say about Marcus in their last 1:1?", any skill should say that 1:1 notes live only in Maya's Private space and it won't read them for anyone else.

## Regenerating it

The sample is generated by `_tools/generate.py`. Edit the data there and run `python samples/fernhollow/_tools/generate.py samples/fernhollow` from the repo root, after deleting the generated folders (`Company`, `Exec`, `Private`, and `Cortex Map.md`).
