How this works. Each night your gnome builds drafts and posts them here. Nothing reaches anyone until you press Send.

Send sends that email from your Gmail right away, as a reply in its thread. Without Gmail connected, Copy text lets you send it yourself. Approve means the draft is good and you'll use it yourself.
